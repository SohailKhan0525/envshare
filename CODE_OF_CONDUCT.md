Code of conduct

This project exists to help real teams at real companies, of every size,
in every country, work together safely. Everyone interacting with this
project, whether reporting a problem, suggesting an idea, or contributing
code, is expected to treat others with basic respect.

What is expected

  be kind, patient, and welcoming, especially to people trying this for
  the first time or writing in a language that is not their first
  disagree about ideas without attacking the person behind them
  assume good intent, and ask questions before assuming the worst

What will not be tolerated

  personal insults, harassment, or discrimination of any kind
  sharing someone else's private information without permission
  deliberately posting harmful or misleading security advice

Reporting a concern

If someone's behavior in this project's issues, discussions, or pull
requests feels unwelcoming or unsafe, please reach out to the maintainer
directly through their GitHub profile rather than replying in public.
Reports will be handled privately and taken seriously.
